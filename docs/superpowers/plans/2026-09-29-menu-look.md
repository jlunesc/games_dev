# Menu look plan (2026-09-29)

Goal: the menus (Menu, Tweak, Stats, Settings, Controller test, Summary) look like the arenas instead of a flat dark page. Owner asked for this on 2026-09-29 and to go ahead without further questions. Looks only: no game, stats or sound change.

1. **Arena behind the menus.** New `src/ui/menu-scene.ts` draws the real arena (sky, drifting layers, embers, floor, the selected boss and the player standing idle) with the existing `drawFrame`, on a full-screen canvas behind the panel. The boss and mood follow the Boss row, so moving the focus on that row changes the backdrop. A new `scene` flag in `FrameLook` skips the health bars and end text. It draws at half the frame rate, stops when a fight is on screen, and follows the Effects switch (Effects off: still picture).
2. **Panel and rows in the arena's colours.** In `src/ui/style.css` the panel becomes a see-through card, the title gets a glowing accent, and rows and the focus mark use the mood's accent colour (set with `style.setProperty`, CSP safe). Text stays readable on top of the picture.
3. **Tests.** `tests/menu-scene.test.ts` (which boss and mood are drawn, no health bars in scene mode, still picture without motion, nothing drawn when hidden).
4. **Docs.** `docs/SPEC.md` (one line), `docs/phone-testing.md` (checklist), `docs/design_history.md`.

Not doing: menu sounds (out of scope), animating the idle figures, per-screen art.
