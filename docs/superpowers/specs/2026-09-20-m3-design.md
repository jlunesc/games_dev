# M3 Design: menu, difficulty dials, summary, settings, stats and export

Approved by the owner in chat on 2026-09-20. Implements milestone M3 of `docs/SPEC.md` section 11, built in two steps: **M3a** (menu, dials and Tweak screen, summary, settings) and **M3b** (stats recording, storage and export). Builds on the M1 and M2 designs. Ideas for later are in `docs/backlog.md`.

## Decisions taken for M3
- **Export (owner):** an Export button opens the phone's share sheet or saves a file; never an upload.
- **Difficulty (owner):** Easy, Normal and Hard are saved sets of dials; a Tweak screen changes each dial. The dials are the owner's list of what makes a boss harder: speed, frequency of attack, hardness to read an attack, health, damage, range, variety of attacks. The dial system must take player traits and environment dials later.
- **Everything else (DELEGATED, tunable):** dial ranges and preset values, menu layout and navigation, what the summary shows, what is recorded, how it is stored.
- The start screen buttons no longer show the button names (owner's note); the start screen becomes the menu.

## M3a: menu, dials, summary, settings

### Screens
All navigate with the d-pad or left stick (up, down, left, right), the bottom button to confirm and the top button to go back, and stay tappable. Holding the top button for a second still leaves a fight.
- **Menu:** the rows are, in order, Fight, Boss (only the Duelist for now, shown by name), Difficulty (Easy, Normal, Hard, or Custom after a tweak; left and right cycles), Tweak difficulty, Settings and Controller test. Fight is first and highlighted when the menu opens. The menu remembers the last boss, difficulty and tweaks, so one bottom-button press from a fresh app start (two after a summary) restarts the same fight.
- **Tweak:** one row per dial with its current value and a short "what it does" line; left and right change it in small steps and up and down move between rows; a "Reset to preset" row. Changing a dial makes the difficulty "Custom" (remembering which preset it started from).
- **Summary** (after a win, a loss, or leaving a fight): result, time, phase reached, hits taken, and the attack that hurt you most; bottom button returns to the menu. Time counts game updates (not the hit-freeze), as the spec says.
- **Settings:** switches for hit freeze, screen shake, flashes and sound; remembered on the device. Switching one off never changes how a fight plays.
- **Fight flow:** menu, fight, summary, menu. A win, a loss and leaving all end in the summary.

### Dials
Each dial is a number where 1 is the boss file as written. Applying the dials produces an adjusted copy of the boss definition (re-checked by the validator); the simulation and the drawing use that copy. The boss file is never changed.

| Dial | What it does | Range (step) |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | Walking and retreating speed and the speed of moves such as the lunge go up; recovery after an attack gets shorter | 0.7 to 1.4 (0.05) |
| Frequency | The pause between attacks is divided by this (higher means more often) | 0.5 to 2.0 (0.1) |
| Readability | The warning (wind-up) of every attack is multiplied by this (shorter is harder to read); the hit windows and moves shift with it; a counterable attack keeps at least the counter window | 0.7 to 1.6 (0.05) |
| Health | Boss health multiplied and rounded | 0.5 to 2.0 (0.1) |
| Damage | Every attack's damage (hits it costs you) multiplied and rounded, at least 1 | 1 to 3 (1) |
| Range | How far attacks reach (hit windows and the distance it starts them from) is multiplied | 0.8 to 1.2 (0.05) |
| Variety | The share of each phase's attacks the boss uses, keeping the most frequent ones (at least one) | 0.5 to 1.0 (0.05) |

| Preset | Speed | Frequency | Readability | Health | Damage | Range | Variety |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | 0.85 | 0.7 | 1.3 | 0.7 | 1 | 0.9 | 0.65 |
| Normal | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Hard | 1.15 | 1.4 | 0.8 | 1.4 | 2 | 1.1 | 1 |

`damage` becomes a field of each attack in the boss file (default 1 when absent). Dial groups (boss now; player and environment later) are data, so a new dial is a new entry, not new screens.

### Code structure
- **Pure, tested:** `src/game/difficulty.ts` (dials, presets, `applyDials`), `src/game/summary.ts` (a tracker that reads the update stream and produces the summary), `src/ui/settings.ts` and `src/ui/prefs.ts` (settings and remembered choices, stored through a small storage interface so tests use memory), `src/ui/nav.ts` (turns d-pad and stick input into one step per press, with repeat while held), and the screen models `src/ui/menu-model.ts`, `src/ui/tweak-model.ts`, `src/ui/settings-model.ts` (what rows exist, what each press does).
- **Browser code:** `src/ui/screens.ts` renders the models as buttons and rows (text only, no `innerHTML`), and `src/ui/app.ts` runs the screens and the fight.
- Input gains up and down (`moveY`); both controller profiles map the d-pad up and down (buttons 12 and 13) and the left stick's vertical axis.
- `hurtPlayer` takes the attack's damage; the fight HUD draws the player's hearts from the player's real maximum.

## M3b: stats recording, storage, export (designed in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-20-m3b-design.md`; the outline below is the starting point)
- **Recorded per fight:** schema version, game version, boss id, seed, dials (preset plus every changed value), date, attempt number, result, duration, phase reached, boss health left, damage dealt and taken, the player's hit timeline, hit accuracy; per boss attack: type, phase, time, distance to the player, outcome (hit, dodged, countered), reaction time from the start of the warning, dodge margin in ms (early or late), damage taken, what the player was doing when hit; behavior: the input log (compact), counts per action, position over time, time at close, mid and far range, punish windows. Derived measures are computed afterwards.
- **Replay:** the seed, dials and input log replay the fight exactly, so the recorder can be checked against a replay and measurements added later without new fights.
- **Storage:** in the browser on the device (IndexedDB), behind a small interface so tests use memory. Android can clear browser data, so the Stats screen reminds the owner to export.
- **Export:** a versioned JSON file, shared through the phone's share sheet where available and saved as a download otherwise. The format is documented in `docs/stats.md`. Exports are git-ignored.

## Tests (M3a)
Dials: every preset and many random dial combinations produce a boss that passes the validator; each dial changes exactly what the table says (speed, frequency, readability shifting hit windows, health, damage, range, variety), Normal changes nothing, and the boss file itself is untouched. Summary: result, time, phase reached, hits and most dangerous attack from scripted fights, including leaving. Settings and prefs: defaults, saving and loading, and bad stored data falling back safely. Navigation: one step per press, repeat while held, no repeat for a single tap. Screen models: focus moves and wraps, left and right change dials within range, presets and Custom behave, Reset works, back and confirm do the right thing. Input: both profiles read up and down. Manual (owner): a checklist in `docs/phone-testing.md`.

## Out of scope for M3a
Stats recording, storage and export (M3b); more bosses; player traits and environment dials; CSV export.

## Done when (M3a)
Tests pass, CI is green and deployed, and the owner has used the menu, tried the presets and the Tweak screen, finished fights on the PC and the S21 and seen the summary, and any changes they asked for are made.
