# M2 Design: boss data format, Ember Duelist, counter, phase 2

Approved by the owner in chat on 2026-09-20. Implements milestone M2 of `docs/SPEC.md` section 11. Builds on `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-20-m1-design.md`. Ideas for later are in `docs/backlog.md`.

## Decisions taken for M2
- **Counter (owner):** an attack press inside a short window near the end of the gold attack's wind-up. No new button.
- **Movement (owner):** the Duelist walks toward the player and keeps its distance, backing off and closing in.
- **Everything else (DELEGATED, tunable):** the owner left the details to Claude. Attack choice is mostly random with rules and a predictability knob; three attacks in phase 1 and a fourth (ground shockwave) in phase 2; about 30 health; a powering-up pause between phases; victory then restart.
- **Bosses are data files** (`src/bosses/*.json`), checked when loaded by a hand-written validator (no dependency). The format can later hold ranges in place of single numbers and an arena section (backlog), but M2 uses single numbers and no arena section.
- The M1 training dummy is removed.

## The fight (what the player sees and feels)
- The Ember Duelist is a glowing fighter, 80 wide and 150 tall, with a visible arm. It starts at x = 960 and has 30 health. Each of the player's hits does 1 damage. It has no body damage.
- **Moving:** between attacks it walks toward the player until it is 200 to 320 units away, backs off if the player is closer than 200, and always faces the player. It waits a gap (70 updates in phase 1) after each attack before choosing the next one, then moves into that attack's range and starts it.
- **Attacks** (each has a wind-up during which the arm shows the pose and the body glows gold or red, then a short active part, then a recovery):

| Attack | Arm pose | Glow | Wind-up / active / recovery (updates) | Starts from | What hurts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ember slam | raised | gold, counterable | 30 / 6 / 30 | 90 to 140 units away | A tall box in front, up to 180 high and 110 out from its body. Cannot be jumped; dash through it or back off. |
| Low sweep | sideways | red, must dodge | 24 / 8 / 24 | 110 to 200 away | A low box 100 high, 210 out. Jump over it or dash through. |
| Lunge | pulled back | red, must dodge | 30 / 10 / 36 | 220 to 320 away | The Duelist rushes 250 units forward for 10 updates; its front is dangerous. Dash through it or get out of the way. |
| Ground burst (phase 2) | arm down | red, must dodge | 30 / 20 / 30 | 150 to 350 away | Five low boxes (50 high) that travel along the floor 540 units outward, one after another. Jump over. |

- **Choosing attacks:** mostly random from the phase's list using weights, with two rules: never the same attack three times in a row, and a "predictability" value (0.25) that makes it follow the list in order that fraction of the time. The random generator is seeded and the seed is kept in the game state, so a fight can be replayed exactly. A new fight (or a restart) uses a new seed.
- **Counter:** during the last 12 updates (0.2 s) of the slam's wind-up, if the player is within 200 units of the Duelist and starts an attack swing, the slam is countered: the Duelist is **staggered** for 90 updates (1.5 s), cannot attack or move, and the player's hits do double damage while it lasts. Too early, too late, too far, or against a red attack, the press is just a normal swing.
- **Phase 2** begins when health falls to 66% of its maximum (19 or fewer). The Duelist stops what it was doing, powers up for 60 updates (glowing, cannot be hurt), then opens with the ground burst. In phase 2 the gap is 40 updates, it walks faster, and after an attack it chains a second attack straight away half the time.
- **Winning:** at 0 health, "Victory" shows and 60 updates later a new fight starts. Losing works as in M1. (The summary screen and menu come in M3.)
- The arena, the player and the controls are unchanged from M1. The start screen offers "Fight the Ember Duelist".

## Boss file format (`src/bosses/ember-duelist.json`, checked by `parseBoss`)
```
id, name, width, height, startX, maxHp
spacing: { min, max }             distance the boss tries to keep while it waits
approachTimeout                    updates it may spend walking into an attack's range before it starts anyway
predictability                     0 = weighted random, 1 = strict cycle through the phase's list
counter: { window, range, staggerTicks, damageMultiplier }
transitionTicks                    length of the powering-up pause between phases
attacks: [ { id, name, pose, class ("counterable" | "mustDodge"),
             windup, active, recovery,
             range: { min, max },   distance from the player at which it can start
             move?: { from, to, speed },   boss moves forward between these attack updates
             hits: [ { from, to, x0, x1, bottom, top } ] } ]   hurt boxes, see below
phases: [ { name, startsAtHpFraction, attacks: [ { id, weight } ], opening?,
            gap, maxChain, chainChance, walkSpeed, retreatSpeed } ]
```
- Times are in updates (60 per second), counted from the first update of an attack (0); a hit box is active for `from <= t < to` and must lie inside the attack's active updates.
- A hit box is measured from the boss's centre in the direction it faces: `x0` to `x1` is the near and far edge, `bottom` to `top` is the height above the floor.
- The validator rejects a broken file with a message naming the exact place (for example `boss.attacks[1].hits[0]: must lie inside the active updates`).
- `docs/bosses.md` documents the format for whoever adds the next boss.

## Architecture changes from M1
- `GameState` replaces `dummy` with `boss` (`BossState`: position, facing, health, phase index, mode, timers, current and pending attack, chain counter, recent attacks, cycle position), and gains `rng` (the generator's state), `seed`, and `endTicks` (replaces `defeatTicks`, used after both defeat and victory). `phase` becomes `'fight' | 'defeated' | 'victory'`.
- `step(state, input, boss)` takes the boss definition as an argument; the definition is never part of the state.
- Boss modes: `gap` (walk and wait), `approach` (walk into range of the chosen attack), `attack`, `stagger`, `transition`.
- New pure modules: `src/game/rng.ts` (seeded random numbers), `src/game/boss.ts` (boss movement, choosing and running attacks), `src/bosses/schema.ts` (types), `src/bosses/parse.ts` (validator), `src/bosses/index.ts` (loads and validates the Duelist).
- Events: `bossHit`, `playerHit`, `dash`, `bossWindupGold`, `bossWindupRed`, `counter`, `phaseChange`, `bossDefeated`, `playerDefeated`. The freeze is longer on a counter (10 updates). Effects and sounds are driven by events, as in M1.
- Drawing: the boss body glows by class during wind-up (gold or red), the arm shows the pose, hurt boxes show while active, staggered is blue, powering up pulses. A health bar with a mark at the phase change replaces the dummy bar.
- The seed for a new fight is chosen in the app (outside the simulation) from the browser's random source.

## Tests
- **Data:** the real boss file parses; each kind of mistake is rejected with the right message.
- **Random numbers:** repeatable for a seed, different for different seeds, always in range.
- **Boss behavior (deterministic, derived from the boss file):** walking to the right distance and backing off, staying in the arena, facing the player; each attack's timing relative to its warning event (the first hurt update is exactly the wind-up later; the active length; the next warning after the recovery and gap); the lunge covers its distance; jumping over the sweep and the burst, dashing through, and the slam's tall box hurting a jumping player; never three of the same attack in a row; predictability 1 cycles in order; same seed same fight, different seed different order.
- **Counter:** works inside the window and in range; not too early, too late, too far, or on a red attack; staggers for the planned length; double damage while staggered.
- **Phases and victory:** crossing the threshold starts the transition once, the boss cannot be hurt during it, it then opens with the phase's opening attack and uses the phase's values; chaining in phase 2; victory at 0 health and the restart with a new seed.
- **Manual (owner):** a checklist in `docs/phone-testing.md` for reading the poses, the counter, phase 2 and winning, on the PC and the S21.

## Out of scope for M2
More bosses, the menu, difficulty presets, the summary screen, stats logging and export (M3), ranges in the boss file and randomized properties, an arena or hazards section, character types (all in `docs/backlog.md`), and any extra player moves.

## Done when
Tests pass, CI is green and deployed, the owner has beaten or lost to the Duelist on the PC and the S21 and reported how it feels, and any tuning they ask for is made in the boss file or `params.ts`.
